📐 Strategy 1: Long-term Reversal (Oversold)

Condition 1 — Trend
Close price below MA(200) — stock is in a long-term downtrend / deep dip
Condition 2 — Oversold
RSI(14) < 30 — stock is in oversold territory (within last 5 bars)
Condition 3 — Confirmation
MACD(12,26,9) line crosses above signal line — momentum turning bullish
Use Case
Contrarian dip-buying — entering during deep corrections when price is cheap and momentum is recovering
Entry
Open price of the next bar after signal bar
Exit
Current date (open position marked-to-market) or cut-loss trigger
